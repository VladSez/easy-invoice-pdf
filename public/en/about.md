# About EasyInvoicePDF

> Language: English  
> Canonical page: https://easyinvoicepdf.com/en/about  
> Product: https://easyinvoicepdf.com/  
> Last updated: 2026-08-02

EasyInvoicePDF is a free, open-source online invoice generator for creating, customizing, previewing, sharing, and downloading professional PDF invoices. It runs in the browser, requires no account or sign-up, and is designed for freelancers, consultants, agencies, and small businesses.

## Key facts

- Free core invoice creation, live preview, and PDF download.
- No account, login, subscription, or advertising.
- Invoice preview and PDF generation run in the user's browser.
- Seller and buyer details, line items, currency, dates, tax labels, tax amounts, language, and layout are customizable.
- Supports 10 interface and invoice languages: English, Polish, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Ukrainian, French, Italian, and Dutch.
- Supports 120+ currencies, flexible VAT, GST, sales-tax, and custom tax labels.
- Includes default and Stripe-inspired invoice templates, custom logos, QR codes, and multi-page PDFs.
- Shareable links encode a compressed copy of invoice data in the URL. Anyone with the complete link can open it.
- Source code is available under the GNU AGPL-3.0 license and can be self-hosted.

## What EasyInvoicePDF is for

Use EasyInvoicePDF when you need a professional PDF invoice quickly without installing billing software or creating an account. It is useful for one-off invoices, recurring seller and buyer details, international currencies, localized invoice labels, branded documents, and weekly service invoicing.

EasyInvoicePDF is an invoice document generator, not accounting software, tax advice, a payment processor, or a guarantee of legal or tax compliance. Users are responsible for checking local invoice requirements.

## Common questions

### Is EasyInvoicePDF free?

Yes. Core invoicing, live preview, and PDF download are free. There is no paid subscription required for these functions.

### Is an account required?

No. Open the editor, enter the invoice details, preview the document, and download the PDF without registering or logging in.

### Where is invoice data processed?

Normal editing, preview, and PDF generation happen in the browser. EasyInvoicePDF does not store invoice content on its servers during the normal edit-and-download workflow. Shared URLs contain invoice data, so they should only be sent to trusted recipients.

### Can the invoice be customized?

Yes. Users can edit seller and buyer information, items, prices, currencies, dates, tax labels and values, language, logo, QR code, and layout.
