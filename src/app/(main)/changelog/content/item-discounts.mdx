## ✨ Highlights

- **Discounts on invoice items** - click **Add discount** under an item's net price and take a percentage (`10%`) or a fixed amount (`50.00 EUR`) off that line. A fixed amount comes off the whole line, whatever the quantity
- **Your own label** - name the discount, e.g. `Charitable donation` or `Loyalty discount`, and it shows on the invoice. Leave it empty and the PDF says "Discount" in the invoice language
- **Tax on the discounted amount** - VAT is calculated after the discount, and the tax summary, totals and amount in words all use the discounted figures. The form shows what comes off and what is left as you type
- **Default template** - a new **Discount** column appears only when an item has a discount, and the label sits under the item name
- **Stripe template** - the item keeps its full price, and a row under it takes the discount off, e.g. `Charitable donation (10% off)  -€1,000.00`. The tax column says what the tax applies to: `23% (on €9,000.00)`

---

### Changed

- Invoices saved in your browser or shared by link before this release open with no discount, so nothing about them changes until you add one
